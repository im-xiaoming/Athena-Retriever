# 2026-10-12 (evening): architecture probes on YouCook2, then all datasets weighted equally

**Question, part 1:** do architecture changes from ActionFormer, TriDet and OV-AVEL raise YouCook2 R@0.5 above the
54-55 plateau (`2026-10-12_seg-plateau.md`)? **Part 2:** the user then dropped the YouCook2-only goal. The main
metric is now **R@0.5 avg**: class-agnostic R@0.5 with K = number of GT steps, averaged over YouCook2 val,
COIN test (seen and unseen pooled), ActivityNet val and HT-Step val. What does the data mix do to it?

Constraint set by the user: nothing may depend on knowing the dataset of a video at inference (production gets one
video, no source label). This rules out per-dataset NMS and the dataset token (`model.source_token`).

## Summary

- None of the architecture changes moves YouCook2 R@0.5 beyond seed noise (±1.5). The Trident boundary head gives
  R@0.7 +1.2 to +2.5 at epochs 2-7; the one-to-one head adds 0.9 R@0.5 on YouCook2 on the same checkpoint but lowers
  ActivityNet.
- Equal shares of the four training sets (`mix_eq`) give the best R@0.5 avg so far: **42.10** vs 41.08 for
  `all_b16`, mainly from COIN (+3.9) and HT-Step (+1.6). One seed, and the best epoch is picked on the same val sets
  (`all_b16`'s checkpoint was picked on YouCook2 R@0.5 only), so the +1.0 is not established.
- Training on HT-Step does not help HT-Step: `mix_noht` (no HT-Step training) scores the same on it.
- ActivityNet stays at 56-58 because of its short events (< 10 % of the video: 21.6 % found), not because of
  overlapping events (86 % found). One global NMS setting looser than the default only loses, on every dataset.

## ActivityNet: why it stays below 60 (`all_b16` best_seg, ActivityNet val, 368 videos, 1,279 events)

GT statistics (val): YouCook2 steps are 3-8 % of the video and never overlap; ActivityNet events are 10 / 25 / 48 %
(p25 / median / p75), 23 % cover half of the video or more, 12 % of event pairs overlap by more than half; HT-Step
steps are 2-7 %.

| ActivityNet events | Share of GT | R@0.5 at K | in the 100 kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| shorter than 10 % of the video | 24.6 % | **21.6** | 83.2 |
| 10-25 % | 26.2 % | 54.6 | 94.0 |
| 25-50 % | 26.4 % | 65.4 | 95.6 |
| 50-90 % | 16.7 % | 83.6 | 99.1 |
| 90 % or more | 6.0 % | 100 | 100 |
| overlapping another event (IoU >= 0.3) | 11.3 % | 86.2 | 97.9 |
| not overlapping | 88.7 % | 53.2 | 92.3 |

The top-K predictions are longer than the GT (median 27 % vs 25 %, p75 64 % vs 48 %). As on YouCook2 (short steps
18 %), the loss is in short events.
