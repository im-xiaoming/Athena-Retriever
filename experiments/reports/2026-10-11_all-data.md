# 2026-10-11: all training sets (`all_b16`), regularisation + augmentation (`all_aug`), text stream (`all_text`)

**Question:** does training on every dataset we have (YouCook2 + COIN + ActivityNet Captions + HT-Step) beat
YouCook2 alone, on YouCook2, on unseen COIN tasks and on the zero-shot benchmark (DCASE, UnAV-100)?

## Summary

- `all_b16` matches the YouCook2-only runs on YouCook2 (segmentation and captions within seed noise) and has the
  best open-vocabulary numbers so far: COIN unseen Acc 12.5 (R1 9.5, R0 7.5).
- Zero-shot benchmark: mAP 23.0 on UnAV-100 (+6.3 over `athena_coin`), 23.1 on DCASE (+1.0, but AP@0.7 3.4 → 7.9).
  UniAV, trained on these sets, is still ~30 mAP points higher.
- `all_b16` overfits: YouCook2 R@0.5 peaks at epoch 8 of 17 (53.98), then falls to 48.8 while the COIN seen accuracy
  (training tasks) keeps rising.
- `all_aug` (10 epochs, dropout, drop-path, EMA, feature augmentation) removes the overfitting: every YouCook2 metric
  still rises or holds at the last epoch. Against `all_b16` (best R@0.5; the rest from each best-CIDEr checkpoint)
  it is equal on segmentation (R@0.5 54.2 vs 54.0, mIoU 51.1 vs 49.5) and COIN unseen (Acc 12.7 vs 12.5, F1 5.0 vs
  4.5), behind on captions (CIDEr 84.6 vs 90.7, txt_sim 0.493 vs 0.510) and grounding (G R1@0.5 44.8 vs 45.7). It
  did not reach the hoped R@0.5 > 55.5.
- `all_text` (the sentence as a third backbone stream, `model.text_stream`; regularisation and 10 epochs of `all_aug`,
  no feature augmentation, at the user's request) is running.
- Not a clean ablation: batch, lr and epochs changed together with the data. The user had no time to rerun the old
  baselines with the new batch / lr.

Reading it:
- No overfitting: train loss stays ~0.4 above `all_b16` at the same epoch, COIN seen accuracy grows slowly (31.5
  instead of 42.6) and the YouCook2 metrics do not fall at the end. The last checkpoint is also the best one, so with
  this recipe the number of epochs no longer has to be tuned.
- Segmentation is the same as `all_b16` at its peak (within seed noise), with fewer epochs.
- Captions are worse at the peak. The weakest part is the top-1 retrieved caption (txt_sim[top1] 0.385 vs 0.448 for
  `all_b16`); the minimum-Bayes-risk pick over the top 20 hides most of it (0.504 vs 0.514). The modality drop and the
  masks make the segment vectors noisier; `aug_modal_drop` is the first candidate to lower.
- Grounding is ~1.5 G R1@0.5 behind.

## Zero-shot benchmark against the original UniAV

Method: `docs/BENCHMARK.md` (class-agnostic: labels dropped on both sides, ActivityNet AP at tIoU 0.1:0.9).
Videos both models have features for: DCASE public_eval 470 of 564, UnAV-100 test 836 of 2167. Our model reads
InternVideo2 + BEATs features, UniAV its ONE-PEACE features. UniAV is trained on these datasets, our model never saw
